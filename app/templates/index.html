Questions, answered

Everything you need to get moving.

Can I use my own branded domain?+

Yes. Workspace administrators can register and verify custom domains, then use them for public short links.

Does OrbitLink support teams?+

Yes. Workspaces include owner, administrator, editor, and viewer roles with permission-aware navigation and actions.

What analytics are captured?+

OrbitLink records click timing, referrer, browser, device, operating system, geography, bot classification, and optional conversions.

Can links be reviewed before publishing?+

Yes. Approval policies can be enabled per workspace for links and campaigns, with a review queue and audit history.
